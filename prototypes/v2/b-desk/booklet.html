A Preliminary Mathematical Exegesis of Diffusion Models

Gaining Clarity From True Understanding · 扩散模型的一个初步数学阐释

Generative AI has transformed countless aspects of our world. Among its many techniques, the diffusion model stands out as a groundbreaking framework—powering innovations like Stable Diffusion, whose magic captivates anyone (myself included) who remembers a time before the AI revolution.

For learners like me, most tutorials and code snippets in blogs and papers provide only fragmented insights—forcing us to piece together scattered, incomplete explanations. I’ve tried to write it like something where ideas build naturally.
